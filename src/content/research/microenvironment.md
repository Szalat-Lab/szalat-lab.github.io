title: Bone marrow microenvironment and treatment response
order: 2
featured: true
summary: >-
  Plasma cell disorders develop within the bone marrow, where immune, myeloid, and stromal cells shape how the
  disease grows and how it responds to therapy. We profile the marrow before and after treatment to understand
  which features of the microenvironment accompany deep and durable responses.
questions:
  - How do immune and myeloid compartments of the marrow change during therapy?
  - Which features of the microenvironment before treatment are associated with response or resistance?
  - How do stromal cells influence the behavior of malignant plasma cells?
approaches:
  - Longitudinal single-cell profiling of paired bone marrow samples
  - Chromatin accessibility profiling (ATAC-seq)
  - Co-culture models of plasma cells and bone marrow stromal cells
  - Computational analysis of cell–cell communication
