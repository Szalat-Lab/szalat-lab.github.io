Raphael Szalat is a hematologist and Director of the Multiple Myeloma Program and the Stem Cell Transplant Program at Boston Medical Center, and Assistant Professor of Hematology and Oncology at the Boston University Chobanian & Avedisian School of Medicine.

He received his MD from Université Paris-Sorbonne and completed a doctorate in hematology at the Dana-Farber Cancer Institute and Harvard Medical School, in collaboration with Université Paris-Sorbonne, followed by a hematology-oncology fellowship at Boston Medical Center.

His clinical work focuses on multiple myeloma clinical trials. His research spans basic and translational studies of multiple myeloma and other plasma cell disorders, including AL amyloidosis and monoclonal gammopathy of clinical significance.
